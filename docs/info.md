## How it works

There are 4 buttons  B1, B2, B3, B4 and 4 Modes M1, M2 , M3, M4

Buttons (B)
B1 use for ON/OFF the device 
B2 use for changing the Mode  
B3 use for increasing frequency
B4 use for decreasing the frequency 
Modes(M)
M1 use to provide  the frequency of 100hz 
M2 use to provide the frequency of 1000hz
M3 use to provide the frequency of 10000hz
M4 use to provide the frequency of 0hz
